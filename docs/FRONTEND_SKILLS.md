## Review method
Inspect the opening, an intermediate scroll state, the workspace, repaired state, phone layout, and reduced-motion state. Check real text and real financial results. Compare source citations with the corresponding elements. Reject clipped artwork, accidental empty sections, unreadable body copy, and generic decoration that does not help the product story.
